# Common Rationalizations

| Excuse | Reality |
|--------|---------|
| "Close enough on spec compliance" | Verify the finding against the contract. A confirmed required gap remains incomplete until corrected; a recorded ruling cannot waive it. |
| "I'll fix it myself, dispatching is overhead" | Controller fixes pollute your context and skip review. Resume the implementer. |
| "One more round will converge" | The cap bounds cost; it does not prove a structural cause. Repeated failure without new evidence requires reassessment before the cap. |
| "The reviewer will just find something new anyway" | Scoped re-reviews verify fixes; they cannot wander. New findings on untouched code go to the ledger, not the loop. |
| "This finding is obviously wrong, I'll drop it" | Check it now against authoritative requirements and code. Reject it with evidence in the ledger or correct the confirmed defect; never silently discard it. |
| "The fix was small, skip the re-review" | Unreviewed fixes are how regressions land. Every round ends with a scoped re-review. |
| "Reviews slow the loop down" | The loop without reviews is just unverified churn. Reviews are the loop's brakes and steering. |
| "Ledger bookkeeping is overhead" | The ledger is what survives compaction. Controllers without one have re-dispatched entire completed task sequences. |
