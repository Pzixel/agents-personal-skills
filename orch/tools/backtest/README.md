Loop-cost gate backtest (numbers in ORIGINS.md, "Loop-cost gate"). Rerun whenever the identity or the `loop_cost` thresholds change. Datasets stay out of git: `python3 extract.py <out>/calls.jsonl` streams every Bash call of the last 14 days from `~/.claude/projects` (about 4 GB read, a few minutes); the loop dataset is the TSV of the looping session (`start_utc, duration_s, agent, agent_type, command`). Then `python3 replay.py <loop.tsv> <out>/calls.jsonl` replays the real policy, and `python3 replay.py <loop.tsv> <out>/calls.jsonl variants` compares the trigger (a) variants.
