Loop-cost gate backtest (numbers in ORIGINS.md, "Loop-cost gate"). Rerun whenever the identity or the `loop_cost` thresholds change. Datasets stay out of git: `python3 extract.py <out>/calls.jsonl` streams every Bash call of the transcripts in `~/.claude/projects` modified in the last 14 days (a long session reaches further back: the 2026-11-02 run held calls of 17 days) (about 4 GB read, a few minutes); the loop dataset is the TSV of the looping session (`start_utc, duration_s, agent, agent_type, command`). Then `python3 replay.py <loop.tsv> <out>/calls.jsonl` replays the real policy, and `python3 replay.py <loop.tsv> <out>/calls.jsonl variants` compares the trigger (a) variants.

Headroom check backtest (numbers in ORIGINS.md, "Headroom check"): with the same `calls.jsonl`, take one snapshot `kubectl --context <prod-context> get pod -A -o json > pods.json` and `kubectl --context <prod-context> top pod -A --containers --no-headers > top.txt`, then `python3 exec_headroom.py calls.jsonl pods.json top.txt` prints the production exec count, the denied count by reason and the denied rows. The snapshot is today's limits and working sets, not those at each exec.

Background-watch backtest (numbers in ORIGINS.md, "Background watch"): `python3 replay.py background-watch [days]` reads the main-session transcripts of `~/.claude/projects` and `~/.claude-accounts/*/projects` directly (extract.py has no notifications) and prints turn ends, fires by branch, pure-wait exemptions, the old-regex delta and the fired list.

Next-step backtest (numbers in ORIGINS.md, "Next step check"): `python3 replay.py next-step [days] [--against <stops.tsv>]` replays the hook's candidate selection (trigger (a) and the session's live mechanisms, no look-ahead) at the first stop of every turn of the main-session transcripts, harness subject directories excluded, and prints turn ends, triggers, candidates per day and each candidate (the set a judge probe on question C runs on), and the CASE-61 witnesses; with the CASE-61 backtest's `stops.tsv` it lists every difference from the look-ahead set with this replay's reason.

Outcomes gate backtest (numbers in ORIGINS.md, "Outcomes line"): with the same `calls.jsonl` (extract.py records `timeout` and `run_in_background`), `python3 replay.py outcomes <out>/calls.jsonl [<start_utc>]` replays `gate_policy.outcomes_decision` on every call and prints fires by trigger (detach, background) and by agent, fires per day, the 10 most recent fires (the launched program, then 300 chars of the command), and the decision on the call whose `start_utc` is given.
