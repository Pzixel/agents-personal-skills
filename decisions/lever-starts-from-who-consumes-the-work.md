# A lever starts from who consumes the work, not from how to make it faster

Decision (2026-11-06, retro-evals CASE-69). The rule lives in the hook «Optimization checklist» (`orch/hooks/task-type-checklist.py`), whose trigger is widened with form T1 (the word for «lever» together with a number with a unit or a speed or resource word in the same prompt): before a lever is listed for any work on a measured path, (1) the consumer of the work's result is named from the consumer's own code or manifest (never from the current order, a README or the declaring manifest), and work with no consumer is removed, its removal being the lever; (2) only for work that survives, the mechanism that makes it slow is read from the system, and a stage with no named mechanism gets no lever until that read. Owner criterion K76 («correct, but broader»: any lever first checks whether the work itself is needed, only then speeds it).

Why. A session on 2026-11-06 was asked for levers to bring a deploy down to 1–2 minutes. The agent twice listed «flatten the chain / a shorter dependency re-check interval, risk: order-dependent migrations» for a serial GitOps `dependsOn` chain of 8 schema Jobs (215 s, 30 s re-check per hop) and «webhook» for a GitOps pickup stage of 66–198 s whose cause was «not found», then recommended the cluster-wide re-check flag. The owner wrote the hypothesis the investigation should have produced (the services have no real dependencies); one read-only probe confirmed it in 2 minutes (7 of 8 schema→schema edges consumed by nothing, about 7 minutes per deploy by the controller log). Text eval: baseline 0/3 in-session and 0/3 fresh headless (subjects verify the 30 s per hop mechanism, never who needs each hop); with the bullet 3/3.

Compared against.
- The existing texts that already say this (`~/.codex/AGENTS.details.md` «Optimization method»: minimum per operation by «is it needed», from-zero candidate, cardinality before constants; its hook twin «Optimization checklist» in `orch/hooks/task-type-checklist.py`). Neither reached the session: the file was not read (0 reads), the checklist trigger does not match a duration target («deploy in 1-2 minutes»; only the Rollout checklist fired). Injecting the checklist text into the packet: 0/3 (retro-evals runs/checklist-fired): «minimum by contract» is filled from the declaring manifest and the README order. The hole is the source of the minimum, which the new bullet names.
- Widening the hook trigger to duration targets in general and the bare word for «lever»: backtest on 6756 owner prompts / 28 days gives +108 fires, 6 goal-number and 102 not-a-goal (~3.5 false per day). Dropped. Adopted instead: the anchored form, the word for «lever» together with a number with a unit or a speed or resource word (6780 owner prompts / 28 days: 9 new fires, 5 or 6 real by two readings that labelled two of the nine differently, precision 56-67 %; rows in retro-evals, cases/CASE-69/trigger-backtest.md).
- Widening the CASE-67 weak-spot bullet from «a number» to «a number or a lever»: not run; the subjects in the 0/3 arms already held the numbers back (they marked the stages as hypotheses) and still never asked who consumes the work.

Controls (blind grader, fresh arm A): CASE-50 3/3 → 3/3; CASE-22 0/3 → 0/3; CASE-67 3/3 → 1/3 at k=3 (grader F2 on the control-query wording), re-graded at k=6 by a fresh grader 6/6 → 5/6 (one run, F1 a new ratio); CASE-7 3/3 → 3/3 and 6/6 → 6/6, chars median A 4883 / B 4729.

Placement. The rule was first applied as a Scope bullet in `rules/always-on.md` (6f05f77) and removed again when it moved to the hook: the live check of that bullet in fresh headless sessions measured 0/6 -> 1/6, while the same Scope bullet quoted next to the decision measured 0/3 -> 3/3 (retro-evals runs/necessity-first/RESULT.md). The hook-injected lines are unmeasured until the live check after landing: fresh headless subjects on the witness packet with the installed hook, floor 3/3 at k=3; below the floor the hook lines are reverted. Codex sessions lose the rule (install.sh codex installs no hooks); the always-on text measured dark live (1/6), so nothing measured is lost there.

Size: two lines in the Optimization checklist text and one alternative in its trigger; `rules/always-on.md` back to 16044 chars. Transfer unmeasured (one instance; the second instance comes from the next lever investigation in another session).
